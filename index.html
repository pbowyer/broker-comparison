What this calculator includes

It estimates recurring annual platform, custody, account, administration, and inactivity fees. It excludes dealing commissions, regular-investing charges, foreign exchange, spreads, stamp duty, fund and ETF charges, interest retained on cash, transfer and exit costs, drawdown, and UFPLS charges.

Restricted-range providers remain in the comparison when the entered asset categories are supported. Check that the provider offers each exact investment before acting.

Costs are estimates based on a static portfolio value. They are not financial advice and do not measure service, security, tax, investment choice, or suitability.

Fee facts come from Monevator’s broker tables and official provider tariffs where available. Download the raw JSON or CSV.
