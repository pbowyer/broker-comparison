# Broker Comparison

## Register

product

## Users

UK investors comparing the recurring platform cost of holding Stocks and Shares ISAs and SIPPs for one person or a household. They may have large portfolios split across funds and several kinds of exchange traded assets. They need to test consolidation choices without learning every provider's tariff first.

## Product Purpose

Calculate and compare annual platform fees using transparent, dated fee rules. The product should identify the cheapest eligible arrangements for keeping all active accounts with one provider, using one provider per person, or assigning each whole account independently. Success means a user can enter a household portfolio, understand the leading options, inspect how each fee was calculated, and distinguish calculation facts from restrictions or non-platform charges.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, candid. The voice is plain spoken and precise. It helps users reason about costs without presenting a cheapest result as financial advice or endorsement.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a sales-led comparison site: no promotional broker treatment, urgency, decorative logos, affiliate-style winner badges, or visual cues that imply endorsement. Do not reduce the decision to an opaque quiz or hide material assumptions behind a simplified recommendation.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with a usable answer, then make every assumption and calculation inspectable.
2. Preserve financial nuance without presenting all detail at once.
3. Treat eligibility, restricted ranges, data dates, and source confidence as part of the answer.
4. Keep household scenarios easy to change so users can compare consolidation choices without re-entering data.
5. Separate factual cost calculation from suitability, risk, and personal financial advice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus states, clear labels and errors, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, semantic structure, and status announcements that do not rely on colour alone. Use plain English and format large monetary values for easy scanning.
