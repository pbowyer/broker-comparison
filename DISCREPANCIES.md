# Differences and unresolved tariff conflicts

Checked 13 September 2026. This file compares the Monevator article dated 4 May 2026 with official provider material available during verification.

## InvestEngine SIPP annual fee — resolved

- Monevator: £0 for the DIY SIPP.
- Older official material: InvestEngine’s 2023 MIFIDPRU disclosure and 2024 SIPP policy said SIPP accounts paid 0.15%, capped at £200 a year.
- Current official tariff: InvestEngine’s customer-facing costs page says ISA and SIPP account fees are both £0 and dates its comparison data 7 July 2026.
- Calculator treatment: InvestEngine is eligible for ETF-only ISAs and SIPPs at £0. The current tariff supersedes the older documents.
- Sources: [Monevator table](https://monevator.com/compare-uk-cheapest-online-brokers/), [current InvestEngine costs](https://investengine.com/costs/), [older InvestEngine disclosure](https://public.investengine.com/pdf/MIFIDPRU-8-Disclosure.pdf)

## HSBC product structure

- Monevator: lists HSBC InvestDirect and HSBC Global Investment Centre as separate services. InvestDirect has a £42 ISA fee and no funds; Global Investment Centre charges 0.25% and is funds-only.
- Official material: HSBC says it is moving InvestDirect customers to Global Investment Centre and adding shares and ETFs to that service.
- Calculator treatment: both Monevator rows remain separate because the official transition page does not provide a complete replacement custody tariff in the extracted evidence. Users should verify which tariff applies to their account.
- Sources: [Monevator table](https://monevator.com/compare-uk-cheapest-online-brokers/), [HSBC transition information](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/investments/existing-customers/shares-on-gic/)

## Fidelity bonds

- Monevator: groups bonds with ETFs, investment trusts, and shares in its table heading.
- Official material: Fidelity says the £90 annual cap applies only to shares, ETFs, and investment trusts. Funds remain subject to its percentage service fee. The page does not identify directly held bonds as capped assets.
- Calculator treatment: Fidelity is excluded when an account contains a non-zero bonds and gilts value.
- Sources: [Monevator table](https://monevator.com/compare-uk-cheapest-online-brokers/), [Fidelity charges](https://www.fidelity.co.uk/services/charges-fees), [Fidelity fee collection explanation](https://www.fidelity.co.uk/markets-insights/personal-finance/personal-finance/how-fees-are-taken-from-your-fidelity-account/)

## Notes that are not discrepancies

- Interactive Investor’s official Core and Plus prices, £100,000 Core limit, five free family members on Plus, and £100,000 Family plan limit match Monevator.
- AJ Bell’s official fund tiers and £42 ISA / £120 SIPP exchange-traded caps match Monevator.
- Hargreaves Lansdown’s official 2026 fund tiers and £150 per-account cap for shares, ETFs, investment trusts, bonds, and gilts match Monevator.
- Vanguard’s official £48 fee below £32,000 and 0.15% fee capped at £375 across a person’s accounts match Monevator.
- Halifax’s official £36 ISA administration fee and 0.25% SIPP fee capped at £198 match Monevator.
